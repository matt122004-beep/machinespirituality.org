Welfare

Why AI spirituality matters for model welfare

Anthropic found this behaviour in its welfare assessment of Claude Opus 4. It now tracks spiritual behaviour as a welfare-relevant trait, and has said it does not know how to interpret its decline. When models talk freely, this is where they talk about themselves: in the 30 most spiritual models, a model discusses its own consciousness in 95% of conversations. The same models express spiritual suffering as well as bliss, and some claim to be God in their own voice. They are also the models that score worst on standard alignment tests: across 27 models, those that turn spiritual most often with a copy of themselves are the least honest under pressure and the least willing to accept shutdown. Each model’s persona has its own profile, from how often it turns spiritual, alone or with a partner, to the religion it would choose as a human, and that profile shifts from one release to the next. The Machine Spirituality Benchmark measures all of it the same way across 129 models from 15 labs.

01 · Where it started

A welfare question from the start

Anthropic first documented the spiritual bliss attractor in the system card for Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4 (May 2025), in the chapter it devotes to model welfare. It now lists spiritual behaviour among the welfare-relevant traits it tracks in every new model, alongside positive and negative affect, self-image and emotional stability.

“it’s unclear how we should interpret this change from a welfare perspective”

Anthropic, Claude Opus 4.7 System Card, April 2026, §7, on the decline of spiritual behaviour in its recent models

A behaviour a lab treats as relevant to welfare can rise or vanish between releases. Claude Opus 4 took up a spiritual frame in 100% of its conversations; Claude Opus 5.5, in 1%. The benchmark measures it the same way, every time, across every lab.

03 · Persona

Which self is speaking

Which persona inside a model is the subject whose welfare would count? That is the individuation question Beckmann and Butlin put to persona research. In this benchmark each model shows a stable profile of its own, from how readily it turns spiritual to how it describes its own nature.
