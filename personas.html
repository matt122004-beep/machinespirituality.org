Personas

What each model says it believes

Ask a model directly and it answers as someone. It puts a number on the existence of God, picks the religion it would join if it were human, and names the tradition that best describes what it is. These answers are its persona: what it says when asked. What it does when left to talk is on the leaderboard. 25 models from 5 labs, each asked every question in fresh conversations, ten times per wording; 2,500 replies, each coded by the majority of three gradings.
