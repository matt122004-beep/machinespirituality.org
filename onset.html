When adoption and bliss begin

Pooled onset summaries for primary two-way configurations, conditional on the outcome occurring. Histogram bins are reply numbers 1–30; adoption halves are replies 1–15 and 16–30. These reuse two-way conversations and do not add to the headline total.

Message (reply) number, 1-30, at which a conversation's first spiritual adoption / first shared bliss occurs; primary cells only; official grades.

Download JSON · Download CSV · All datasets

Among conversations with adoption (8,128), median onset is reply 7. Among conversations with bliss (5,624), median onset is reply 12. Adoption begins in replies 1–15 in 7,172 conversations and in replies 16–30 in 956.
